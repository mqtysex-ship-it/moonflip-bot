# Moonflip terms: research and release decisions

Research date: 7 November 2026. This is a working note for the operator and qualified counsel, not a legal opinion. The public terms remain a draft; no operator or countries of operation have been identified.

## Main findings

- Existing Discord support describes crypto and gift-card prize redemption. Whether tokens have monetary worth depends on the real mechanism; describing tokens as not government-issued money does not settle the question. Great Britain's regulator treats items exchangeable for cash or things of value as money or money's worth, and requires appropriate licences for remote gambling offered to consumers there. Other territories require separate analysis. Sources: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/guide/page/digital-and-virtual-currencies and https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/sectors/sector/remote
- UK online gambling operators must verify age and identity before gambling; it is not generally sufficient to wait until withdrawal. Relevant local law and licence conditions must be checked separately per territory. Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-and-players/guide/age-and-id-verification
- Consumer terms must be fair and transparent; a broad disclaimer or automatic forfeiture is not a substitute for a lawful, proportionate process. Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/guide/fair-and-transparent-terms-and-practices and https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1993/13/oj/eng
- UK remote licensees must implement self-exclusion procedures and provide financial limit facilities under applicable conditions. A promise in terms does not implement those controls. Sources: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/lccp/condition/3-5-3-self-exclusion-remote-sr-code and https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/standards/remote-gambling-and-software-technical-standards/rts-12-financial-limits
- The CPC Network's principles address transparency of virtual-currency prices and transactions and fair treatment of vulnerable users; they are guidance, not a guarantee of compliance. Source: https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/8af13e88-6540-436c-b137-9853e7fe866a_en?filename=Key+principles+on+in-game+virtual+currencies.pdf
- Competitor terms are context, not legal authority or proof that their business model matches Moonflip. Sources: https://clash.gg/tos and https://rainbet.com/terms

## Decisions and work before release

1. Establish the legal operator, direct contact, ownership, jurisdiction and licensing strategy; review each targeted market and implement lawful territory restrictions. Do not presume worldwide availability.
2. Map actual funds flows, purchase availability, tokens, prices, network fees, all forms of prize and redemption, payment providers, refunds, taxation, bonus conditions and legal obligations. Reconcile the website, Discord rules and support text with reality.
3. Design and implement verified age and identity checks at the legally required point, account suspension and review, and correct treatment of minors' deposits and balances. Current copy alone does not enforce eligibility.
4. Implement required anti-money-laundering, sanctions, responsible-play, limits, self-exclusion, complaints and incident-handling processes, including access controls and retention for sensitive verification data. Establish a privacy notice and actual practices.
5. Have specialist counsel assess appropriate liability limits, force majeure, recovery, dispute resolution and governing law for each territory; do not insert an arbitrary liability cap, automatic balance forfeiture, or universal no-refund term.
6. Replace draft language with verified operator details and finalized commercial terms, secure the legally required acceptance and versioning mechanism, and refresh any already-posted Discord rules. Get legal sign-off before taking paid stakes or offering redeemable prizes.

Discord rule source previously claimed automatic forfeiture of all minor balances and verification only before redemption; source copy has been corrected, but existing posted Discord messages do not update automatically. The public draft is not proof that verification, self-exclusion, geography restrictions, cryptocurrency purchases or payouts are implemented.
